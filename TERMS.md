## 1. What the app is

UNI Made EZ does two things. It turns **your own course material** into flash
cards, a summary, the key information and a quiz; and it carries **courses
written out in full** — the explanation, worked examples and practice — that
somebody wrote for it rather than a machine extracted. Both are done by a study
engine built into the page, running on your device in your browser. There is no
account, no server that holds your material, and no live AI call while you
study.

## 2. What it costs

Nothing. It is offered free, as is, and may be changed or withdrawn at any time.

## 3. Your material stays on your device

Your files, the text pulled out of them, your cards, your ratings and your
progress are held in your browser's own storage on the device you are using.
They are not uploaded, not collected, and not visible to JohnsonXCorp.

The page reaches the network in the following cases, and **none of them carries
your material**:

| When | What is sent | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Every page load | Nothing but the request for the page itself. The fonts are inside it — no font service is contacted | GitHub Pages |
| Every time the app is opened, at most once an hour | A random id for this browser, how many times the app has been opened, how many subjects were started, whether you signed up, your device family and your time zone. No IP address is stored, and nothing about your files | The publisher's database, in Canada |
| Only if you **sign up** | The name and email you type, when you press the button | The publisher's database, in Canada |
| Only if you set a subject to **Other** and answer the optional question | The subject name you type, up to 40 characters, with nothing beside it — no id, no time, nothing that could link it to you | The publisher's database, in Canada |
| Only if you press **Send it** in the suggestion box | The kind of note, your words, the app version, and an email only if you typed one. No id, nothing from your files. Read only by JohnsonXCorp | The publisher's database, in Canada |
| Only if you paste a **web address** to read | That address only | Wikipedia's API for a Wikipedia article; otherwise the site itself, and if it refuses, the r.jina.ai reader service |
| Only if you use the **scan reader** the first time | Nothing — it downloads the reader (~15 MB) | cdnjs, jsDelivr, tessdata.projectnaptha.com |
| Only if you use **Dictate** | The audio of your voice, only after you say yes to the dialog | Your browser's own speech service |

The first row is the whole of what happens if you only ever study. Everything
below it is something you chose to do.

There are no cookies, no advertising identifiers, no analytics service, no
third-party script of any kind, no location and no fingerprinting. Nothing is
sold and nothing is shared with anybody. The full account, field by field, is in
the app at **#/rights** and in the Privacy Notice.

Clearing your browser's site data deletes everything held on your device. Use
**Backup & restore** to keep a copy. Installing the app to an iPhone home screen
gives it storage separate from Safari's — the app says so where it matters.

## 4. Signing up

Signing up is **optional and unlocks nothing**. Every course, card, summary,
quiz and export works identically whether you sign up or skip, and you are asked
once. Earlier versions put Summary and Quiz behind it; that was removed in
v2.27, because access to study material should not be the price of your contact
details.

If you do sign up, your name and email are stored in Canada, used only to know
who is using the app and to write to you about it — not sold, not shared, not
added to any list you cannot leave. Ask and they are deleted, within 30 days.

## 4a. The suggestion box

The **Suggest an idea** button sends a note to JohnsonXCorp and to nobody else —
nothing is published. Please do not put anything in a note you would not want
the publisher to read, and do not use it to send course material. An email is
optional; add one only if you want a reply. Ideas you send may be used to
improve the app, without payment or credit, and sending one creates no
obligation on either side.

## 5. What the app does not promise

Material built from **your own files** is **extracted, not understood**. The
engine finds definitions, figures, steps and terms by pattern, and it makes
characteristic mistakes: a table read as a sentence, a "not" carried into the
wrong half, an aside taken for a definition. The app flags what it is unsure of
and shows you the original passage — check it.

The **written courses** are a different kind of thing: they are authored, and
every numeric answer in them is recomputed by a computer algebra system before
the page is published. They are still a study aid. They follow the chapter and
section order of the text a course is set from so a lesson matches what you
would read beside it, and each lesson names that reading — but a lesson
consolidates neighbouring sections, so match them to your text by topic rather
than by number. Nothing in them is copied from any textbook or past paper.

It is a study aid, not a source. It is not a substitute for your course
material, your instructor, or your own reading, and no mark or academic outcome
is promised. Anything you submit for credit is your responsibility, including
compliance with your institution's academic-integrity rules.

The app is provided **as is**, without warranty of any kind. To the extent the
law allows, JohnsonXCorp is not liable for any loss arising from its use,
including lost study data.
