## 1. What the app is

UNI Made EZ turns your own course material into flash cards, a summary, the key
information and a quiz. The work is done by a study engine built into the page,
running on your device in your browser. There is no account, no server that
holds your material, and no live AI call while you study.

## 2. What it costs

Nothing. It is offered free, as is, and may be changed or withdrawn at any time.

## 3. Your material stays on your device

Your files, the text pulled out of them, your cards, your ratings and your
progress are held in your browser's own storage on the device you are using.
They are not uploaded, not collected, and not visible to JohnsonXCorp.

There are exactly four times the page reaches the network, and none of them
carries your material:

| When | What is sent | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Every page load | Nothing but the request for the page and its fonts | GitHub Pages, Google Fonts |
| You paste a **web address** to read | That address only | Wikipedia's API, or the r.jina.ai reader service |
| You use the **scan reader** the first time | Nothing — it downloads the reader (~15 MB) | cdnjs, jsDelivr, tessdata.projectnaptha.com |
| You use **Dictate** | The audio of your voice, only after you say yes to the dialog | Your browser's own speech service |

Clearing your browser's site data deletes everything. Use **Backup & restore**
to keep a copy. Installing the app to an iPhone home screen gives it storage
separate from Safari's — the app says so where it matters.

## 4. Signing up

Summary and Quiz ask for a name and an email once. That opens a message in your
own mail app, pre-addressed to the publisher, which you choose to send or not.
Nothing is transmitted until you send it. The address is used to know who is
using the app and to reach you about it — not sold, not shared, not added to any
list you cannot leave. Ask and it will be deleted.

## 5. What the app does not promise

The study material is **extracted, not understood**. The engine finds
definitions, figures, steps and terms by pattern, and it makes characteristic
mistakes: a table read as a sentence, a "not" carried into the wrong half, an
aside taken for a definition. The app flags what it is unsure of and shows you
the original passage — check it.

It is a study aid, not a source. It is not a substitute for your course
material, your instructor, or your own reading, and no mark or academic outcome
is promised. Anything you submit for credit is your responsibility, including
compliance with your institution's academic-integrity rules.

The app is provided **as is**, without warranty of any kind. To the extent the
law allows, JohnsonXCorp is not liable for any loss arising from its use,
including lost study data.
